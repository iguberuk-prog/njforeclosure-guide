# Brief for a corrected video

Hand this to whoever produces the animation. Everything below is copy-exact.
Nothing in the "say this" column is a suggestion; it is the string that must
appear, character for character.

---

## Why this brief exists

Four assets have now come back with the business name spelled wrong, each time
differently:

| Asset | What it printed | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Whiteboard infographic | `njforclosureguide.org` | `njforeclosureguide.org` |
| Video 1 | `njforclotureguide.org` | `njforeclosureguide.org` |
| Video 2, at 0:01 | `FORECLASUNCE` | `FORECLOSURE` |
| Logo file | `NJ FORECOLSURE GUIDE` | `NJ FORECLOSURE GUIDE` |

Video 1's URL does not resolve. Anyone who typed what they saw reached nothing.

Whatever tool is generating these renders text as shapes rather than as words,
so no spellchecker catches it and it fails a different way each run. Every
frame containing text has to be read by a human before delivery.

## Text that appears on screen

Only these strings. Copy and paste them; do not retype.

```
FORECLOSURE
www.njforeclosureguide.org
Free 6-question assessment
See your options
Keep it or sell it
Start free
```

Buttons, labels and captions may use only the strings above. Video 2's button
currently reads `Boingttes`, which is not a word.

---

## Two claims that have to change

**"Free 30-Second Assessment" is wrong.** The assessment is six questions and
takes about two minutes. Use `Free 6-question assessment`. Overstating speed on
the one thing a viewer can check in ten seconds is a bad trade, and the site
says two minutes everywhere else.

**The ending cannot be a for-sale sign.** Both videos currently resolve to
`List your home`, `Strong Offer!`, and a For Sale sign. Every outcome shown is
a sale. The site's central promise is the opposite: if keeping the home is
realistic we say so first, and point people at a free HUD counselor or the
state mediation program before any sale route.

An ad that promises a sale, pointing at a site that leads with keeping the
home, converts the wrong people and reads as bait to the right ones.

**Replace the final beat with a fork, not a sign.** One path labelled
`Keep it`, one labelled `Sell it`, both drawn equally, then the URL. The
message is "you have options," not "we will buy your house."

## The story, beat by beat

| # | On screen | Text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worried homeowner, letter in hand | `FORECLOSURE` |
| 2 | They open a laptop | `www.njforeclosureguide.org` |
| 3 | Six checkboxes ticking | `Free 6-question assessment` |
| 4 | Path forks into two equal branches | `Keep it or sell it` |
| 5 | Same person, calmer, holding a plan | `See your options` |
| 6 | End card | `www.njforeclosureguide.org` and `Start free` |

No dollar signs, no confetti, no For Sale sign, no green checkmark over a
price. This audience is being circled by people promising exactly that.

## Format

Two exports, not one. The current files are 720x1280, which is a phone format
and will not sit on a web page.

**For social** (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook)
- 1080x1920 vertical, 8 to 15 seconds
- Legible with the sound off. Most viewers never turn it on.
- Burned-in captions if there is narration

**For the website**
- 1920x1080 horizontal, or 1080x1080 square
- 45 to 90 seconds, which is long enough to actually explain the NJ timeline
- Delivered as MP4 (H.264) plus a WebM, and a poster frame as JPG
- Under about 8MB, or it slows the page down more than it helps
- A caption file (.vtt), which is both an accessibility requirement and
  something Google can read
